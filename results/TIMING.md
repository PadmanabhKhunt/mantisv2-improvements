## 3. Main points

- LoRA is the most expensive method: 4.3 h in total, against 14 min for the mixer head and 3.3 h for the paper's RF.
- On the big Sleep-EDFx sets the RF takes about an hour (3,753 s on SEDFx-ID); the mixer takes 4 minutes and is more accurate.
- The slowest single run was LoRA on PCL-OOD (48 channels): 5,075 s = 85 min for 3 seeds.
- Extracting features on CAP took 56 min on the CPU in the reproduction notebook; the same kind of step takes 2–3 min on the T4 for much bigger datasets (SEDFx-ID: 138 s), so use a GPU.
